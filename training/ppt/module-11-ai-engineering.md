# Module 11 PPT | AI-Assisted Software Engineering

**README duration:** 10 h | **Build thread:** use AI to improve a real engineering task, then verify it | **Evidence:** prompt framework, reviewed result, and risk checklist

## Slide sequence

1. **AI changes the workflow, not accountability** (5 min) — Human owns requirements, review, tests, and release.
2. **Context before clever prompts** (15 min) — Task, audience, repo facts, constraints, examples, output format; distinguish known facts from assumptions.
3. **Ground and manage context** (15 min) — Provide relevant sources, summarize selectively, ask for citations to supplied context; avoid dumping secrets or whole repos.
4. **Agents need bounded work** (15 min) — Planner/researcher/implementer/reviewer roles, explicit inputs/outputs, approval gates, stop conditions.
5. **Architecture support, not authority** (15 min) — Ask for options and trade-offs; compare with requirements, operational constraints, and team knowledge.
6. **Review generated output** (20 min) — Run tests, inspect diff, threat-model input, check licenses/privacy, evaluate hallucination and bias.
7. **Measure useful impact** (10 min) — Compare time-to-verified-result, rework, defects, learning; run Demo 11, then Lab 11.

## Instructor cues

Use an institution-approved AI tool and a synthetic code snippet. If AI is unavailable, groups manually evaluate the supplied sample output. Do not present agents or productivity gains as guaranteed; define human approval before code, data, or deployment changes.
