# Module 10 PPT | Interview and Placement Readiness

**README duration:** 6 h | **Build thread:** explain work clearly and honestly | **Evidence:** practiced coding, project pitch, and portfolio checklist

## Slide sequence

1. **An interview is a working conversation** (5 min) — Clarify, think aloud, test, reflect; asking a question is professional.
2. **Solve with a repeatable method** (15 min) — Restate, examples, constraints, simple solution, test, improve; arrays/strings/hash basics.
3. **Explain a project with evidence** (15 min) — User problem, architecture, personal contribution, trade-off, result, next step.
4. **Behavioral answers without scripts** (15 min) — Situation, task, action, result; use a real example and own your role.
5. **Portfolio should be verifiable** (10 min) — README, screenshots, run steps, tests, deployment, meaningful commits; never claim team work as solo work.
6. **Practice and feedback** (10 min) — Run Demo 10, then Lab 10. Feedback: one strength, one actionable next step.

## Instructor cues

Use supportive, time-boxed mock interviews. Give students quiet think time and permit pseudocode before syntax. Do not promise placement outcomes. Encourage honest descriptions of AI assistance and team contributions.
